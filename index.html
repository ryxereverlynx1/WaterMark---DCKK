Privacy & Local Processing

Your images stay strictly on your device. Watermarking, preview generation, and full-resolution exports happen entirely within your local browser.

  • Zero Remote Uploads: No image data, metadata, or coordinates are ever transferred to a remote server or cloud service.
  • Client-Side Engine: Uses standard browser HTML5 Canvas 2D and File APIs.
  • Memory Cleanliness: Temporary object URLs and image buffers are promptly disposed of when replacing or clearing images.
  • Original Quality Guaranteed: Preview uses a lightweight cached canvas for immediate 60fps interactivity, while the exported image is rendered at 100% of the original photo's natural width and height.
